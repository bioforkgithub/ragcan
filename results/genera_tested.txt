How to read it
  genomes      genomes selected for that genus
  gtdb_gen     how many GTDB genera those genomes fall into (1 = GTDB keeps the genus whole)
  bins         RaGCAn's answer at 65% AAI on the genomes that GTDB also classifies, which is
               what the scoring uses (1 = coherent, more = proposed division)
  all_bins     RaGCAn's answer on ALL the genomes screened. Where it differs from bins (marked *),
               RaGCAn set apart a genome that GTDB does not classify, so that flag was never scored.
               Those flags are listed in results/unscored_flags_ncbi_check.tsv.
  result       agree-whole    both keep it whole
               agree-divide   both divide it   (+ = divided exactly as GTDB does)
               missed         GTDB divides it, RaGCAn does not
               flagged        RaGCAn divides it, GTDB does not
               not scored     ran, but fewer than 3 of its genomes are in GTDB
  GTDB family  the family most of its genomes belong to in GTDB
